Opening Opul for the first time

Three steps, once.

  1. Move it to Applications

    Open Opul.dmg from your Downloads folder, then drag Opul onto the Applications folder in the window that appears.

  2. Open Opul

    Open Opul from Applications. Opul isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS says it can't verify the app. Close the warning, and don't choose Move to Trash.

  3. Allow it in System Settings

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click Open Anyway next to the message about Opul. Confirm when macOS asks again. It may ask for your password or Touch ID.

That's it. From now on Opul opens normally and installs its own updates.
