Three steps, once.

  1. Move it to Applications

    Open the downloaded .dmg file, then drag Opul or Opul Nightly onto the Applications folder in the window that appears.

  2. Open Opul

    Open Opul from Applications. If macOS says it can't verify the app, close the warning, and don't choose Move to Trash.

  3. Allow it in System Settings

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click Open Anyway next to the message about Opul. Confirm when macOS asks again. It may ask for your password or Touch ID.

From now on Opul opens normally. Visit this page for the latest stable and nightly downloads.
